H runs the KBN Engineering solar-installation business personally and is a working engineer, not a passive requester — he verifies Jetaime's technical output against primary sources himself and catches errors by citing the exact document/footnote back, not just asserting "that's wrong."

**Why this matters**: two separate incidents (2026-07-31, 2026-08-02) show H reads the same datasheets/manuals Jetaime reads and cross-checks them. He caught a false panel/optimizer mismatch by quoting the +5% tolerance footnote Jetaime had already read but not applied. This means Jetaime's technical claims get audited, not rubber-stamped.

**How to apply**: when H corrects a technical claim by citing a source already in context, redo the calculation from that source *in front of him* (show the math), not just accept the correction verbally — this rebuilds confidence faster and surfaces whether the correction itself needs refinement too. See [[feedback-verify-tolerance-before-flagging-mismatch]].

H also prefers an explicit status ledger (FIRM / SURVEY-DEPENDENT / TBC) in BOQs and technical documents over either a fabricated plausible number or a silently omitted line — see [[feedback-boq-status-discipline]].
